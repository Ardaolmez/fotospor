## Translation (English)

**Samba in the Beşiktaş goal: Gabriel Brazao**

Beşiktaş, who are searching for a goalkeeper, have once again moved on Gabriel Brazao — a name that came onto their agenda during the winter break. The club is reportedly set to sit down with Santos shortly.

One of the Eagle's goalkeeper targets during the winter transfer window was Gabriel Brazao, who is playing for Santos. When an agreement on the transfer fee could not be reached with the Brazilian side, the move was put on hold. However, it has emerged that the Black-and-Whites will move again shortly.

€15 MILLION ASKING PRICE

The Brazilian goalkeeper has a contract with Santos Club until 31 December 2028. The 25-year-old is reportedly open to a move to Beşiktaş. A €15 million transfer fee is being talked about for the 1.92-meter shot-stopper. The Black-and-Whites will negotiate to bring the figure down.

SET FOR YEARS

Gabriel Brazao, who joined Santos from Inter on a free transfer in February 2024, had been on the rise again in his home country. If Beşiktaş can complete the signing, they could be set in goal for years. Brazao is 25, an age that is still considered "young" for a goalkeeper.
