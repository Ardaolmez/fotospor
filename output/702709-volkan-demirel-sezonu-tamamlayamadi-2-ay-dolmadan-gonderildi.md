## Translation (English)

**Volkan Demirel could not finish the season! Sacked in less than two months**

Volkan Demirel — the legendary captain of Fenerbahçe and the Turkish national team — has not been able to carry the brilliant success of his playing career into his career as a manager. The young manager failed to deliver the expected results at his latest post, Gençlerbirliği, and was dismissed before even two months had passed since he took charge.

Gençlerbirliği, one of the clubs in the Trendyol Süper Lig, have parted ways with manager Volkan Demirel. Demirel, who took charge of the Ankara side just two months ago, was relieved of his duties with two matches remaining in the league.

In matchweek 32 of the Süper Lig, Gençlerbirliği had visited Karagümrük and lost 1-0 to their bottom-placed opponents, leaving them 17th on 28 points. In a critical match, the decision to let play continue through a position where Gençlerbirliği were appealing for a goal had caused controversy. The following statement was issued by the capital club:

THANK YOU, VOLKAN DEMİREL

As a result of the decision of the Gençlerbirliği Sports Club Board of Directors and the talks held with him, we have parted ways with our manager Volkan Demirel. We thank Mr. Demirel and his coaching staff for the contributions they made to our club during their time in the role, and we wish them every success in the rest of their careers. — Gençlerbirliği Sports Club
